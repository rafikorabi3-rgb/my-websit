OUR STORY

50+ YEARS OF GAMING

From a garage in California to global icon

1972

ATARI IS BORN

Nolan Bushnell and Ted Dabney founded Atari in Sunnyvale, California. Pong was released the same year, becoming the first commercially successful video game.

1975

HOME PONG

Atari releases a home version of Pong. Sears sells 150,000 units in the first holiday season alone, proving the viability of home gaming.

1977

ATARI 2600 LAUNCH

The Atari 2600 (originally VCS) launches at $199. With a cartridge-based system, it becomes the best-selling game console of its era.

1982

PEAK & CRASH

Atari revenues hit $2 billion — then the video game crash of 1983 shook the entire industry. Poor game quality and market saturation took their toll.

1989

LYNX & JAGUAR ERA

Atari launches the Lynx (world's first color handheld) and later the Jaguar, claiming it was the first 64-bit console — pushing hardware to its limits.

2022

ATARI REBORN

The Atari VCS launches, a modern re-imagining of the classic console. The brand lives on through retro collections, new games, and a passionate global fanbase.
